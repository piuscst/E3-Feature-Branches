# Portrait plan

Question: what should this character's single default portrait look like?

Character: an excited trading-card collector who just pulled a very rare card.

## A: Geometry (explored on branch A)

- Shows: about 100+ small star and sparkle shapes all over his face, like a shiny effect
- Arrangement: scattered all over his face
- Impression: thrilled, like he just pulled a very rare card worth a lot

## B: Assets (explored on branch B)

- Image 1: a card sleeve and a top loader held up on the left side of his face, I will get images from the internet
- Image 2: the very rare card on the right side of his face, I will also get the very rare card picture from the internet
- Impression: reads as a collector that has gear to store his card

## C: Color and Curves (explored on branch C)

- Gradient: a background that will feel heavenly, yellow to white gradient
- Bézier curve: a wide grin that curves up at the corners
- Impression: excited and magical

## Choosing

Criterion: which version best shows the excitement of the rare card pull
